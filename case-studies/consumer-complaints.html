The business problem

Complaint records are detailed, high-volume, and difficult to interpret quickly. Decision-makers need a clear view of where complaints are increasing, which products and issues create the greatest pressure, and whether companies are responding on time.

The project converts complaint-level public records into an analytical workflow and interactive dashboard designed for quicker investigation and evidence-based recommendations.

What I built

Reliable data pipeline

Combined CFPB extracts, standardized fields, removed duplicate complaint IDs, validated date coverage, and documented data-quality checks.

Advanced SQL analysis

Used CTEs, conditional aggregation, rankings, window functions, year-over-year comparisons, and a three-month moving average.

Business investigation

Analyzed product concentration, common issues, late-response rates, company performance, state patterns, and the January 2025 spike.

Interactive delivery

Built a responsive Streamlit and Plotly dashboard with filters, navigation, light and dark modes, KPIs, maps, tables, and downloadable visuals.

Key findings

Credit cards led complaint volume.

They represented 38.3% of the selected complaints, making them the largest product category in the analysis.

Company response performance was generally strong.

99.3% of complaints received a timely response, while approximately 0.7% were answered late.

January 2025 was an unusual peak.

Complaints increased from 14,524 in December 2024 to 31,564 in January 2025, a month-over-month increase of approximately 117%.

Geographic concentration matters.

California recorded the highest state-level complaint volume, supporting the need for location-specific monitoring.

Recommendations

  1. Prioritize root-cause analysis for credit-card and account-management complaints.
  2. Introduce automated alerts for unusual month-over-month complaint increases.
  3. Monitor high-volume companies alongside late-response rates instead of judging performance by volume alone.
  4. Use state-level complaint rates with population or customer counts when internal exposure data becomes available.
  5. Refresh the pipeline regularly so the dashboard supports ongoing operational review.
