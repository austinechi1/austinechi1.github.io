StreamWave Content Performance Analytics

Problem

Which titles, genres, devices, and regions drive reach, engagement, and modeled revenue?

Process

Cleaned 15,035 records, built SQL analyses, explored trends in Python, and translated findings into recommendations.

Tools

PostgreSQL, Python, Matplotlib

02 · Business Intelligence

UK Online Retail Performance Dashboard

Problem

How can retail transaction data reveal sales performance, customer value, product trends, and the business impact of returns?

Process

Cleaned and classified transactions in Power Query, built a data model and DAX measures, then designed a three-page interactive report.

Tools

Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Data Modelling

Results

Revealed £9.77M net revenue, approximately 20K orders, 4K active customers, and an 8.4% return rate.
